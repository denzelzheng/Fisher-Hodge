# Notes

The phosphorylation cycle with every hyperedge mass action in the activity and the regular-solution excess free energy G = χ₁ c_X c_Xp + χ₂ c_Xp c_E per site, χ₁ = −0.15 (attractive), χ₂ = 0.15 (repulsive).

## Choice of χ

The design started at χ = ±0.3. The reference data then reached c = 34, so χ was set to ±0.15 before any model was trained. On the training states χ₁² c_X c_Xp ≤ 0.28, inside the convex range (below 1). Two test sets cross it: the stronger potential reaches 6.9 and the raised concentrations 2.6, with local accumulation up to c = 23.6 and 12.2. The trajectories stay bounded.

## Test sets

Training conditions, drive ΔG 3–4, a stronger and finer potential U, X and Xp at the start × 1.5, and the 16×16 grid.
